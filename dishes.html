Ginisang Monggo with Chicharong Baboy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup mung beans (monggo)
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon fish sauce (patis)
  • 1 to 2 cups spinach or malunggay leaves
  • 1 cup chicharong baboy
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Procedure

  1. Wash the mung beans and boil them in 4 cups of water until soft.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and sauté the garlic, onion, and tomatoes.
  3. Add the fish sauce and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Pour the sautéed mixture into the cooked mung beans.
  5. Simmer for 5 to 10 minutes.
  6. Add the spinach or malunggay leaves.
  7. Mix in half of the chicharong baboy.
  8. Season with salt and pepper.
  9. Top with the remaining chicharong baboy before serving.
  10. Serve hot with steamed rice.
